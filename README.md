# Bouquet Scratch

A little scratch-off ticket you can play in your browser.

👉 **[Open it](https://gygabo-r.github.io/bouquet-scratch/)**

A gold foil panel covers a hidden seasonal picture. Drag across it with your
mouse or finger to scratch the foil away and reveal what's underneath — when
you've cleared enough, you get a little confetti celebration. Tap **"I want new"**
for a fresh ticket with a different picture.

It's a web app, so there's nothing to download. If you'd like quick access, you
can add it to your home screen / install it from your browser like any other app.
